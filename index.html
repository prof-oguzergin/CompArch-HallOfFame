Acceptance Rates. Submissions and acceptance rates for HPCA, MICRO, ISCA, and ASPLOS over the years. Sources: the program chairs' messages in the proceedings, SIGARCH trip reports, csconferences and OpenAccept. Submissions are the counts the program chairs report as received. Since 2023 ASPLOS reviews submissions in two or three cycles a year. A paper accepted after a major revision counts in the year it was submitted, even when it is presented at the next year's conference. ASPLOS 2024, for example, accepted 170 papers before its conference and 24 revisions after it.
